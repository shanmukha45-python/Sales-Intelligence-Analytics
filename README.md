\# 📊 Sales Intelligence Analytics Dashboard



An end-to-end \*\*Sales Intelligence and Business Analytics project\*\* built using Python, SQL, SQLite, Pandas, Plotly, and Streamlit.



The project analyzes sales, profit, customers, products, regions, categories, discounts, and business performance through an interactive dashboard.



\---



\## 🚀 Project Overview



This project transforms raw sales transaction data into actionable business insights.



The workflow covers:



\- Data inspection and quality validation

\- Exploratory data analysis

\- SQL-based business analysis

\- KPI calculation

\- Customer and product analysis

\- Profitability analysis

\- Interactive Streamlit dashboard

\- Dynamic business filters

\- Downloadable filtered data



\---



\## 🎯 Business Objectives



The dashboard helps answer questions such as:



\- What are the total sales and profit?

\- Which categories generate the most revenue?

\- Which regions are most profitable?

\- Which products generate the highest profit?

\- Which products are loss-making?

\- How does profitability vary across sub-categories?

\- What are the monthly sales and profit trends?

\- How do different discount levels relate to profitability?

\- Which customers generate the highest sales and profit?
